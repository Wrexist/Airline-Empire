Privacy policy

Your airline lives on your device. We do not run advertising, tracking or analytics services in the game.

Game data and preferences

Saved airlines, fleets, routes, finances and game history are stored in the app's private device storage. Sound, haptic and interface preferences, plus the history used to limit Pro offers, are stored in private app preferences. This data is not sent to a developer server. You can export a campaign backup to a destination you choose through the system file picker. A copy saved to a cloud storage provider follows that provider's policies. Apple device backups may include it according to your settings. Deleting the app removes its local data.

Purchases

Optional Pro purchases use Apple's StoreKit service. Apple processes payments and provides the app with verified purchase information so it can unlock the features you own. We do not receive your payment card details. Purchasing, restoring purchases and refreshing App Store information may require an internet connection. See Apple's privacy policy for Apple's handling of this information.

Tracking and developer collection

The app contains no advertising SDK, analytics SDK, developer account system or developer telemetry service. We do not collect gameplay or device identifiers through the app. The privacy manifest declares private UserDefaults access for preferences and offer history, and declares no tracking.

Who is responsible?

Airline Empire is provided by Isac Molin. For privacy questions or requests about information you have sent us, email isacmolin@gmail.com.

Support and external links

If you choose to email support, we receive your email address, message and any attachments. We use these to respond, investigate your issue and maintain necessary support records. We keep correspondence only as long as needed for these purposes and any applicable legal obligations. You can ask us to delete your correspondence. GitHub issue reports are public and subject to GitHub's privacy policy. Do not include payment details, passwords or other private information in a public report. External pages are handled by their respective providers.

This website

This support website does not embed advertising, analytics scripts or contact forms. The hosting provider processes connection information, such as IP addresses and request logs, to deliver and secure these pages. Email and external services have their own privacy policies.

Changes

This page and the App Store privacy details will be updated if the app's data practices change.
