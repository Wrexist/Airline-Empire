Privacy policy

Your airline lives on your device. We do not run advertising, tracking or analytics services in the game.

Game data and preferences

Saved airlines, fleets, routes, finances and game history are stored in the app's private device storage. Sound, haptic and interface preferences, plus the history used to limit Pro offers, are stored in private app preferences. This data is not sent to a developer server. Apple device backups may include it according to your settings. Deleting the app removes its local data.

Purchases

Optional Pro purchases use Apple's StoreKit service. Apple processes payments and provides the app with verified purchase information so it can unlock the features you own. We do not receive your payment card details. Purchasing, restoring purchases and refreshing App Store information may require an internet connection. See Apple's privacy policy for Apple's handling of this information.

Tracking and developer collection

The app contains no advertising SDK, analytics SDK, developer account system or developer telemetry service. We do not collect gameplay or device identifiers through the app. The privacy manifest declares private UserDefaults access for preferences and offer history, and declares no tracking.

Support and external links

If you choose to contact us through support, we can see what you submit. GitHub issue reports are public and subject to GitHub's privacy policy. Do not include payment details, passwords or other private information in a public report. External pages are handled by their respective providers.

Changes

This page and the App Store privacy details will be updated if the app's data practices change.
