Privacy policy

Airline Empire collects nothing, sends nothing, and has nowhere to send it.

The short version

The app has no account system, no analytics, no advertising, no third-party SDKs and no network code of any kind. It does not ask for your name, your email, your location, your contacts, your device identifiers or your photos, and it has no server to store them on if it did. You can confirm this by playing it in Airplane Mode: nothing about the game changes.

What the app stores, and where

Your saved games are written to the app's own private storage on your device — the airline you founded, its network, fleet, finances, reputation and history. That data never leaves the device except through mechanisms you control yourself, such as an encrypted device backup you have configured with Apple. Deleting the app deletes the saves with it.

Data collected

None. The app's App Store privacy details declare no data collection of any kind, and its bundled privacy manifest declares no tracking domains and no use of required-reason APIs.

Children

Because nothing is collected, nothing is collected from children either. The app contains no advertising, no purchases, no chat, no user-generated content and no external links out of gameplay.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app ever collects anything — it has no plans to — this page will say so before that version ships, and the App Store privacy details will be updated in the same release.

Contact

Questions about this policy can go to the support contact listed on the support page.
