What kind of game is this?+

An airline management game, built around routes, aircraft, finances and competition. You run the business and make the decisions; this is not a flight simulator.

What can I play for free?+

Start with the Founder scenario, one campaign, the 20 airports nearest your home and the first two eras. The core economy, fleet management and AI competition are included.

What does Pro add?+

All 94 airports, all five eras, every starting scenario and unlimited campaign saves. Choose a weekly or yearly auto-renewing subscription, or a one-time Lifetime purchase. All three provide the same Pro access. No paid currency or shortcuts.

Can I play offline?+

Yes. Campaigns run offline with local saves and no game account. Purchases, restoring Pro and external links require a connection. Export backups to protect your saves; there is no automatic cloud save sync.

When can I download it?+

Airline Empire is preparing for its App Store launch on iPhone and iPad. Availability will be confirmed on the App Store once the game is released.
