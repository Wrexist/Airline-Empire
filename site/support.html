Support

Everything the game does happens on your device, which makes most questions answerable from here.

Getting started

  1. Found an airline: name it, pick a starting scenario and a home airport.
  2. Buy an aircraft. Used aircraft are available immediately; new ones are orders with a delivery lead time.
  3. Open a route — the dashboard suggests two, ranked by demand — then assign your aircraft to it from the route's detail screen.
  4. Set the speed and watch a day run. Money moves; a statement closes at the end of each month.

Common questions

My route is losing money. Open it and read the breakdown. The usual causes are a fare far from what the market will bear, an aircraft too large for the demand, or a competitor that has just entered the same market.

Everything flies full at any price. That should no longer happen — if it does, please report it, because it is the exact shape of a bug that was fixed before release.

Where are my saves? In the app's own private storage on your device. Multiple slots are supported, and the game autosaves when it goes to the background. Deleting the app deletes the saves.

Can I play offline? That is the only way it plays. There is no online mode, no account and no sync.

Will there be an Android version? Nothing is announced.

Reporting a bug

The most useful report names the device and iOS version, what you expected, what happened instead, and — because the simulation is deterministic — the seed and scenario the game was started with. With those, a run can be reproduced exactly.
