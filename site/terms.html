Terms of Use

Airline Empire is free to start, with optional Pro access.

App license

The App Store version uses Apple's Standard Licensed Application End User License Agreement. That agreement governs the license to use the app.

Pro options

Pro Weekly and Pro Yearly are auto-renewing subscriptions. Pro Lifetime is a one-time non-consumable purchase. All options unlock the same Pro features. Prices, billing periods and any eligible introductory offer are shown in the app before purchase and confirmed by Apple.

Subscriptions

Payment is charged to your Apple Account. Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the current period ends. Renewal is charged within 24 hours before the end of the current period. An introductory offer, when eligible, changes to the displayed standard price after its stated period.

Manage or cancel through Apple Account subscriptions. Cancelling keeps access through the paid period. After expiry, existing saved airlines and operations remain available while new expansion follows the free-tier limits. A verified Apple billing grace period preserves access until its deadline.

Restoring and help

Use Restore purchases in Settings or the Pro screen with the Apple Account that made the purchase. Apple handles refund requests. Game saves remain local to the device; restoring Pro does not restore a deleted save.
